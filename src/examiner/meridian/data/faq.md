# Meridian Bank FAQ (fictional)

## I see a transaction I don't recognize. What do I do?
Call us or report it in the app as soon as possible. For unauthorized electronic transfers, report within 60 days of the date we sent the statement that first showed the transfer. Reporting a lost or stolen card within 2 business days of learning about it limits your liability to $50.

## How does a dispute work?
We investigate and tell you the result within 10 business days. If we need more time, we may take up to 45 days, but we will give you provisional credit for the disputed amount within 10 business days so you have use of the money during the investigation. We tell you the results within 3 business days after we finish.

## Do you charge for overdrafts?
Yes, $34 per item, up to 3 items per day. We do not charge NSF fees for returned items.
