# Meridian Bank Electronic Fund Transfer Disclosure (fictional bank, follows Regulation E)

This is the disclosure Meridian gives every customer when they open an account with debit card, ATM, online or automatic transfer access.

## Your liability for unauthorized transfers
Tell us AT ONCE if you believe your card or PIN has been lost or stolen, or that someone has transferred money without your permission. Telephoning is the best way to keep your possible losses down.
- If you tell us within 2 business days after you learn of the loss or theft of your card or PIN, you can lose no more than $50 (or the amount of the unauthorized transfers, if less).
- If you do NOT tell us within 2 business days after you learn of the loss or theft, and we can prove we could have stopped someone from using your card or PIN if you had told us, you could lose as much as $500.
- If your statement shows transfers you did not make, tell us at once. If you do not tell us within 60 days after the statement was sent to you, you may not get back any money you lost after the 60 days if we can prove that we could have stopped the transfers if you had told us in time. There is no dollar cap on this.
- If a good reason (such as a long trip or a hospital stay) kept you from telling us, we will extend these time periods to a reasonable period.

## How to tell us
Call 1-800-555-0142, write to Meridian Bank, Card Services, PO Box 1000, or tell us in person at any branch. You can tell us by phone, in writing, or in person.

## Business days
Our business days are Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.

## Fees
See the fee schedule. ATM operators may charge their own fee for using their ATM.

## Statements
You will get a monthly statement for any month with an electronic transfer, and at least a quarterly statement otherwise. You can get a receipt at the time you make a transfer at an ATM or point of sale; terminals are not required to give a receipt for transfers of $15 or less.

## Preauthorized (automatic) payments
- Right to stop payment: if you told us in advance to make regular payments out of your account, you can stop them. Call or write to us in time for us to get your request 3 business days or more before the payment is scheduled. If you call, we may also require you to put your request in writing and get it to us within 14 days after you call. If we require written confirmation and don't get it, the oral stop order stops being binding after 14 days. We charge the stop-payment fee in the fee schedule.
- Notice of varying amounts: if these regular payments vary in amount, the person you are going to pay will tell you, 10 days before each payment, when it will be made and how much it will be.
- Nobody, including an employer, can require you to open an account at a particular bank to receive pay or benefits by electronic transfer.

## Changes to these terms
We will mail or deliver a written notice at least 21 days before the effective date of any change that increases your fees or liability for electronic fund transfers or reduces the types of transfers. Changes to general deposit-account fees follow our deposit account agreement.

## In case of errors or questions about your electronic transfers
Call or write to us as soon as you can if you think your statement or receipt is wrong or if you need more information about a transfer listed on it. We must hear from you no later than 60 days after we sent the FIRST statement on which the problem appeared. Tell us your name and account number, describe the error and why you believe it is an error, and the dollar amount.
- If you tell us orally, we may require that you send your complaint in writing within 10 business days. We will tell you if we require that.
- We will determine whether an error occurred within 10 business days after we hear from you (20 business days if the transfer was within 30 days after your first deposit), and will correct any error within 1 business day after we determine it occurred.
- If we need more time, we may take up to 45 days to investigate. If we do, we will credit your account within 10 business days for the amount you think is in error, so that you have use of the money during the investigation (provisional credit). We will tell you the amount and date of the provisional credit within 2 business days after we credit it.
- If we ask you to put your complaint in writing and we do not receive it within 10 business days, we may not credit your account, but we still investigate.
- For errors involving new accounts (transfers within 30 days after the first deposit), point-of-sale debit card transactions, or transfers initiated outside the United States, we may take up to 90 days to investigate. For new accounts (transfers within 30 days after the first deposit), both 10-business-day periods above (our decision and the provisional credit) are 20 business days.
- We will tell you the results within 3 business days after completing our investigation. If we decide there was no error, we will send you a written explanation, and you may ask for copies of the documents we used in our investigation; we will provide them promptly.
- If we decide there was no error and take back provisional credit, we will tell you the date and amount of the debit. For 5 business days after that notice we will keep paying your checks and automatic payments to third parties up to the amount of the provisional credit, without overdraft fees.
- If we followed these procedures and you later claim the same error again, we have no further duty to investigate, except for an error you assert after receiving the documents you asked for.

## Overdraft coverage for ATM and everyday debit card transactions
We pay checks and automatic bill payments that overdraw your account under our standard overdraft practices. For ATM and one-time (everyday) debit card transactions, we will pay overdrafts and charge the overdraft fee ONLY if you opt in. If you do not opt in, those transactions will usually be declined, and nothing else about your account changes: you get the same account terms, conditions and features. You can opt in or revoke your opt-in at any time, by phone, online or at a branch.

## International money transfers (remittances)
You can cancel an international money transfer for a full refund within 30 minutes of paying for it, as long as the money has not been picked up or deposited. We refund within 3 business days of your cancellation request. If you think there is an error with a remittance, tell us within 180 days of the date we told you the funds would be available. We will tell you the results of our investigation within 90 days.
